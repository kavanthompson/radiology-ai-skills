# Core Radiology – Pediatric Imaging

**Source**: *Core Radiology: A Visual Approach to Diagnostic Imaging*, 2nd Edition (Sun, Shi, Mandell), Chapter 14 – Pediatric Imaging.

**Purpose**: Actionable frameworks for residents. Working toolkit for neonatal emergencies, congenital anomalies, and age-specific differentials.

---

## 1. Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Neonatal Chest – Respiratory Distress Differential
- **TTN** (transient tachypnea): fluid in fissures, hyperinflation, resolves in 24–48 h
- **RDS** (surfactant deficiency): low volume, granular opacities, air bronchograms (preemies)
- **Meconium aspiration**: patchy bilateral opacities, hyperinflation, term/post-term
- **Neonatal pneumonia**: similar to meconium or patchy; group B strep classic
- **Congenital diaphragmatic hernia**: bowel in chest, contralateral mediastinal shift
- **Congenital pulmonary airway malformation (CPAM)** / sequestration

### Pediatric GI Emergencies
- **Midgut volvulus / malrotation**: upper GI – DJ junction not at left of spine / not at level of duodenal bulb; corkscrew or duodenal obstruction
- **Intussusception**: target / doughnut sign on US; air or hydrostatic enema reduction
- **Pyloric stenosis**: “olive,” elongated pylorus >14–16 mm length, wall >3–4 mm on US
- **Appendicitis**: same criteria as adults but higher rate of perforation in young children
- **NEC**: pneumatosis, portal venous gas, free air (surgical)

### Germinal Matrix Hemorrhage (Premature Infants)
- Grade I: confined to germinal matrix (caudothalamic groove)
- Grade II: intraventricular without ventriculomegaly
- Grade III: intraventricular with ventriculomegaly
- Parenchymal hemorrhagic infarction (venous) – previously called “Grade IV”

## 2. Neuro Developmental Anomalies

- **Schizencephaly**: full-thickness cleft lined by gray matter (open- or closed-lip)
- **Lissencephaly / pachygyria / polymicrogyria**: migration disorders
- **Holoprosencephaly**: failed hemispheric cleavage (alobar → semilobar → lobar)
- **Chiari II**: small posterior fossa, tonsillar herniation, myelomeningocele association
- **Dandy-Walker**: vermian hypoplasia + cystic 4th ventricle enlargement

---

## 3. Pediatric MSK & Variants

- **Salter-Harris fractures** (I–V); II most common
- **Toddler’s fracture**: nondisplaced spiral tibial fracture
- **SCFE** (slipped capital femoral epiphysis): Klein’s line, frog-leg views
- **Legg-Calvé-Perthes**: avascular necrosis of femoral head
- **Osteomyelitis / septic arthritis**: urgent; US for effusion, MRI for marrow
- Normal variants: physiologic periostitis of newborns, metaphyseal spurs, etc.

## 4. Classic Signs & Patterns

| Sign / Pattern              | Meaning                                      | Importance |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------|
| **Double bubble**           | Duodenal atresia                             | Associated with Down syndrome |
| **Corkscrew**               | Midgut volvulus                              | Surgical emergency |
| **Target / doughnut**       | Intussusception                              | US diagnosis |
| **Olive**                   | Pyloric stenosis                             | Clinical + US |
| **Pneumatosis + portal gas**| NEC                                          | Medical vs surgical |
| **Caudothalamic groove bleed**| Germinal matrix hemorrhage                 | Premature infants |

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns & Core Exam Traps

- Do not miss malrotation on upper GI – DJ junction position is critical.
- Intussusception reduction is both diagnostic and therapeutic; know contraindications (perforation, peritonitis).
- Physiologic periostitis of the newborn is bilateral and symmetric – do not call child abuse.
- “Grade IV” germinal matrix hemorrhage is actually venous infarction, not simple extension.
- Always consider non-accidental trauma in young children with fractures of varying ages or unusual locations.
