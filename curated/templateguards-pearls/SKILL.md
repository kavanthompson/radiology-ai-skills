# TemplateGuards ABR Pearls Skill

High-yield, 1-line ABR Core pearls embedded in the 7 TemplateGuards on-call fallback templates.
Use this skill when ResidentShield or any ambient AI agent needs contextual teaching lines during dictation or case review without interrupting workflow.

## Source
- Live templates: https://github.com/kavanthompson/TemplateGuards/tree/main/templates
- Demo: https://template-guards.vercel.app

## Pearls (copy-ready)

### Chest XR
Fleischner Society guidelines for incidental pulmonary nodules: solid <6 mm generally no follow-up in low-risk; 6–8 mm CT at 6–12 months.

### Head CT / Stroke
ASPECTS / early ischemic signs: loss of gray-white differentiation, hyperdense MCA, insular ribbon sign.

### Abdomen/Pelvis CT
Appendicitis secondary signs: periappendiceal fat stranding, appendicolith, wall thickening >6 mm, free fluid.

### MSK XR (ankle/knee/wrist)
Bone tumor matrix: chondroid (arcs/rings), osseous (cloud-like), fibrous (ground-glass).

### PE CTA
RV strain signs: RV/LV ratio >1, septal bowing, reflux into IVC/hepatic veins.

### C-Spine XR (trauma)
NEXUS / Canadian C-spine + instability signs: prevertebral soft-tissue widening, fanning of spinous processes, facet distraction.

### Non-contrast Abdomen XR
Free air / SBO secondary signs: Rigler sign, football sign, air-fluid levels >3, string-of-pearls.

## Usage for agents
When a resident is dictating or reviewing one of the above study types, surface the matching 1-line pearl optionally (ABR Teaching Mode). Keep friction zero — never force display during high-volume call.
